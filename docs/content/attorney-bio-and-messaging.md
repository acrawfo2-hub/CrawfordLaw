### Headline

### Professional summary
Employment and labor counsel with a decade of in-house experience advising senior leadership at global Fortune 100 and high-growth technology companies. Andrew’s practice focuses on workplace investigations, accommodations and leaves compliance, labor relations, and employer-side counseling — informed by years of owning those problems inside the business, not only from outside counsel.

### Narrative
At **Deere & Company**, Andrew served as Labor Relations Manager for North American Parts Operations, providing legal and policy guidance on employee relations, discipline, and employment compliance for more than 1,200 employees across unionized and non-union distribution centers. He directed grievance resolution and arbitration preparation with UAW-represented locations, led pandemic response compliance, and negotiated the 2021 collective bargaining agreement covering 10,000 unionized employees. Earlier, as Employee Relations Manager across manufacturing sites, he led investigations and compliance work for 1,000+ employees and designed incentive programs that improved productivity while reducing pay grievances.

In Central Texas, Andrew served as **HR & Employment Law Manager** at The Boring Company — advising on FLSA, FMLA, safety, and compliance, and writing the employee handbook during rapid scaling — and as **HR Manager and Legal Advisor** for Activision Blizzard King (a division of Microsoft), supporting QA and Infinity Ward Studios through merger-related policy integration, labor relations, and first-contract bargaining for more than 700 represented QA employees.

He holds a J.D., Magna Cum Laude, from the University of Illinois College of Law (2014), is licensed in Texas and Illinois, and brings SPHR-level HR strategy to every engagement. He previously served as a commissioned officer in the U.S. Army National Guard, culminating as a Company Commander.

## Tone rules (professional look in copy)

**Do**
- Lead with Fortune 100 / Deere / Microsoft-scale facts and concrete headcounts
- Use calm, precise employer language (compliance, privilege, process, documentation)
- Separate “schedule” (phone / AI assistant) from “start a file” (structured intake)

**Don’t**
- Co-brand with finance or imply a joint firm
- Inflate titles or imply outcomes/guarantees
- Lead with hobby/personal filler on the About page
- Publish the personal Gmail as the firm contact
- Overuse buzzwords (“synergy,” “one-stop,” “full-service everything”)

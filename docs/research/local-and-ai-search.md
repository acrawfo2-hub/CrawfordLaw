# Research: Getting Noticed in Leander, TX — Web Search & AI Search

**Market:** Leander / Cedar Park / Liberty Hill / Georgetown / Round Rock / greater Austin (Williamson & Travis Counties)  
**Positioning challenge:** Employer-side employment specialty + local general practice

---

## Executive summary

Visibility for Crawford Law will come from **three parallel systems**:

1. **Google Local / Map Pack** — Google Business Profile (GBP), NAP consistency, reviews, proximity.
2. **Organic SEO** — practice-area page architecture, Texas-specific content, E-E-A-T.
3. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** — schema, answer-first FAQs, entity consistency, AI crawler access so ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews can cite the firm.

Leander is a growth market (Williamson County suburb; local SEO vendors note rapid population growth and rising competition). Employment counsel for *employers* is less crowded than plaintiff employment in Austin metro, but pure “employment lawyer Leander” queries still mix plaintiff and defense intent — **copy and schema must make employer-side identity unmistakable**.

## 1. Local search (Google Map Pack)

### Why it matters
For “near me” and city-qualified queries (“employment attorney Leander”, “lawyer near me”, “estate planning attorney Cedar Park”), the Map Pack captures a large share of clicks. Website SEO alone cannot replace GBP.

### Priority actions (non-negotiable)

| Action | Detail for Crawford |
|---|---|
| Claim & verify GBP | Name: **Crawford Law PLLC** exactly as SOS filing |
| Primary category | Prefer **Employment Attorney** (specialty signal) |
| Secondary categories | Attorney / Business Attorney / Estate Planning Attorney as fits actual offerings — don’t spam unrelated categories |
| Services list | Investigations; FMLA/ADA leave & accommodations; workplace training; employment policies; business formation; contracts; family law; estate planning — each as discrete services |
| NAP consistency | Same Name / Address / Phone everywhere (site footer, GBP, Avvo, Justia, State Bar, Chamber) |
| Photos | Real attorney, office/meeting space, Leander context — not only stock |
| Posts | Weekly: Texas employment updates, training offers, process explainers |
| Reviews | Ask every appropriate client; aim for velocity + mentions of service type & city |
| Q&A | Seed common employer questions (investigation timing, FMLA interactive process, etc.) |

### Citation / directory stack (Texas solo baseline)
- State Bar of Texas attorney profile + firm listing
- Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Martindale, Lawyers.com
- Apple Business Connect / Bing Places
- Leander / Cedar Park / Williamson County Chamber (if joining)
- Local sponsorships / CLE / SHRM chapter speaking → local backlinks

### Local page strategy (avoid thin doorways)
Do **not** mass-produce empty “Attorney in X city” pages. Prefer:

1. **One strong Service Area page** covering North Austin / Williamson County with real local proof (courts, industries, employer base, commute/business corridors).
2. **Practice pages that naturally include Leander + metro language** where accurate.
3. Optional deeper pages only when you can add unique substance (e.g., “Workplace investigations for Williamson County employers”).

## 2. Organic SEO for employment + general practice

### Keyword reality
Employer-side queries are often **problem-shaped**, not “hire a lawyer”-shaped:

- “workplace investigation attorney Texas”
- “FMLA leave compliance employer”
- “ADA accommodation interactive process Texas”
- “HR harassment investigation outside counsel”
- “employment law training for managers Texas”

General / local queries:

- “business formation attorney Leander”
- “estate planning lawyer Cedar Park”
- “family law attorney Leander TX”
- “contract review lawyer near me”

### Content architecture that ranks
From employment-lawyer SEO guides ([WebKey](https://webkeydigital.com/employment-lawyer-seo/), [LexBlog / YMM](https://www.lexblog.com/2026/07/01/seo-for-employment-lawyers-6-step-blueprint-to-get-cases/)):

1. **Hub pages** for each core service (Investigations, Leave & Accommodations, Training, Employer Counsel).
2. **Spoke guides** answering one question deeply (Texas-specific statutes, TWC/EEOC process, timelines).
3. **Attorney bio** with credentials, sameAs links, knowsAbout topics.
4. **Internal links** from guides → hubs → Start a File / Schedule.

### E-E-A-T for a new solo firm
New firms lack case history. Compensate with:
- Detailed bio (experience narrative, bar admission, jurisdictions)
- Process transparency
- Texas statute/court references on practice pages
- Bylined articles on LinkedIn / JD Supra / local business press
- Speaking / training calendars (creates authority + backlinks)
- Clear disclaimers (no outcome guarantees)

## 3. AI search / Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Sources: [Subscribe PR — 2026 AEO checklist](https://subscribepr.com/blog/the-2026-aeo-checklist-for-law-firms/), [Custom Legal — AEO for Lawyers](https://custom.legal/law-firm-seo-that-works/answer-engine-optimization/), [Lawless Clicks — Schema for AI citations](https://lawlessclicks.com/ai-geo-optimization-for-attorneys/schema-markup-law-firms-ai-search-citations-2026/).

### How AI discovery differs from classic SEO
AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Copilot) typically **name a few firms** in a synthesized answer. Winning is about being **extractable, entity-clear, and credible** — not only ranking #1 in blue links.

### Crawford AEO launch checklist (highest leverage)

**Crawl & technical**
1. Allow GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended in `robots.txt`
2. XML sitemap → Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools
3. Fast mobile Core Web Vitals; HTTPS; clean semantic HTML

**Schema (JSON-LD)**
4. `LegalService` / `LocalBusiness` for the firm (NAP, `areaServed`, `serviceType`)
5. `Person` for Andrew Michael Crawford (bar credentials, `alumniOf`, `knowsAbout`, `sameAs`)
6. `FAQPage` only on real Q&A blocks
7. `BreadcrumbList` on interior pages
8. Stable `@id` for the organization entity across pages

**Content shape**
9. Practice pages lead with an **80–120 word direct definition** of the service
10. Cover: what it is / who it’s for / process / engagement expectations
11. **6–10 FAQs per practice page**, buyer-phrased, 40–60 word extractable answers first
12. At least one **Texas-specific citation** per employment page (statute, agency, or court)
13. Visible “Last updated” dates; refresh quarterly
14. Put FAQs on the practice page — not only on `/faq`

**Entity & reputation**
15. Identical NAP everywhere
16. GBP + Avvo + State Bar `sameAs` links
17. Reviews that mention practice type + geography when natural
18. Quarterly press / bylined content / speaking mentions to build co-citations

### Example AI-target questions (employment)
- “Do Texas employers need an outside attorney for a harassment investigation?”
- “What is the ADA interactive process for employers in Texas?”
- “How long does an FMLA leave eligibility review take?”
- “What should an employer handbook say about accommodations?”
- “Workplace investigation attorney near Austin / Leander”

### Example AI-target questions (general / local)
- “How do I form an LLC in Texas?”
- “Do I need a will if I live in Williamson County?”
- “What should be in a small business contract in Texas?”

## 4. Competitive landscape notes (Austin / Central Texas)

Observed competitive set (not exhaustive):

| Type | Examples | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Employer-side boutiques / groups | Treaty Oak; Lloyd Gosselink employment group; national firms (e.g. Littler) | Compete on **responsiveness, niche (investigations + leave), local accessibility, pricing clarity** |
| Plaintiff employment firms | Kaplan and others | Do not compete on their messaging; disambiguate employer counsel on every page |
| General practice solos | Many thin template sites | Opportunity: professional IA + specialty depth will outclass most local generalists |

**Whitespace:** A Leander-based solo who is *clearly* the local option for **employer investigations + FMLA/ADA/leave counseling + training**, while also being the neighborhood attorney for formations/estate/family — if IA keeps the specialty sharp.
